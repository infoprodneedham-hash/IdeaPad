Built for Pure Creativity & Visual AI Exploration

IdeaPad was created as a lightweight, frictionless companion for visual creators, writers, designers, and hobbyists experimenting with Generative AI models.

Visual AI Prompter

Primarily designed for generating nuanced visual prompts for Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT. Stack lens types, camera angles, color stories, and art styles without memorizing complex syntax.

Local & Private

No external server saves your ideas. Every visual node, moodboard entry, and preset stays entirely in your browser's local sandbox.

1-Click JSON Transfer

Switching from desktop to tablet or phone? Simply download your stored JSON snapshot file with one click and load it into any device.

Just For Fun

Not working on an enterprise prompt? Use IdeaPad to doodle concepts, explore visual inspirations, design moodboards, or just play around with imaginative ideas.

Important Notice & User Responsibility

Your Data is in Your Hands: Because IdeaPad does not maintain any accounts, cloud databases, or centralized backups, it is solely the user's responsibility to periodically download and save their JSON snapshots before clearing browser cookies or changing machines.
