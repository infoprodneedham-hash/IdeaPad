How To Make The Best Use of Drawing Board

Drawing Board Pro is designed to eliminate writer's block when crafting complex visual prompts. Follow this quick 3-step walkthrough:

Step 1

Compose Visual Nodes

Use layers, draw freely, consider output choices, set artistic moods visually. Experiment with text, freehand, line tool and snap to grid options.

Step 2

One click download jpeg or png file

You can also save your workspace to a slot. Grab your optimized prompt image from your downloads folder tuned for Gemini, Claude, or ChatGPT.

Step 3

Backup JSON Snapshot

Upload your visual prompt to your favourite ai and dont forget to export your workspace snapshot file to sync between devices without creating an account.

Built for Pure Creativity & Visual AI Exploration

Drawing Board was created as a lightweight, frictionless companion for visual creators, writers, designers, and hobbyists experimenting with Generative AI models.

Visual AI Prompter

Primarily designed for generating nuanced visual prompts for Gemini, Claude, and ChatGPT. Stack lens types, camera angles, color stories, and art styles without memorizing complex syntax.

Local & Private

No external server saves your ideas. Every visual node, moodboard entry, and preset stays entirely in your browser's local sandbox.

1-Click JSON Transfer

Switching from desktop to tablet or phone? Simply download your stored JSON snapshot file with one click and load it into any device.

Just For Fun

Not working on an enterprise prompt? Use Drawing Board to doodle concepts, explore visual inspirations, design moodboards, or just play around with imaginative ideas.
